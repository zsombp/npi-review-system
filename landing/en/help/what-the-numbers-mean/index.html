    What the numbers in the daily report mean

    • The daily report
    • 3 min read

    The daily report comes as two messages. The first holds the numbers, the second the analysis, if reviews with text arrived that day.

    What counts as new

    • A review is new when the guest posted it in the 24 hours before the report.
    • Google sometimes releases a review late. Then the first report after it arrives counts it, up to seven days after it was posted.
    • Every review is counted by exactly one report. If a report is made again, it gives the same number.
    • If the guest rewrites the review, we do not count it again. A separate part shows the change, see: Changed and removed reviews.

    For each venue

    • The number of new reviews and their average.
    • The star breakdown, from five down to one.
    • The venue's Google rating and its total number of reviews.
    • A red mark if a one-star or two-star review arrived.

    Venues with no new review get one line each at the end of the list. Below them, the “Total” line gives the numbers for the whole account.

    At the end of the report

    • “Unanswered negatives”: reviews of three stars or fewer, at most 14 days old, that have waited at least a day. We do not see a reply to them yet. The oldest comes first, and each has a link to the review.
    • “No fresh data”: the venues where the download failed or was skipped. The next successful download makes up for them.
    • If no new review arrived at any venue, the report flags it as a warning. At a smaller venue this is common, and it does not mean anything is wrong.
    • If you connected Tripadvisor, a separate line shows the new reviews there. They are not in the Google numbers.

    The analysis

    The second message is made from the day's reviews with text. The “Mentions by name” part lists the staff guests named, and the “Themes” part what came up more than once. If no review with text arrived that day, this message is not sent.

    The same numbers on the Today tab

    On the Today tab, The period in numbers card works from the same daily numbers. You choose the period with the Today, 7 days and 30 days buttons. The Full breakdown by star table shows the stars for each venue.
