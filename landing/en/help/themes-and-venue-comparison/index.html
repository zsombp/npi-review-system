    Themes and comparing your venues

    • Analysis
    • 6 min read

    The Kimutatások tab, for analysis, shows the numbers for a period, venue by venue. At the top, choose 7, 30 or 90 days, or one of the last 3 calendar months, listed by name.

    A full month compares with the month before. The current month compares with the same number of days of the previous month. The period control shows those dates. Your choice stays for the session. On the dishes and staff views, a selected month counts as 30 days.

    The tab counts by the date Google gives each review. Ma, the morning screen, counts by the days of the morning reports, so the two can differ by a few reviews. Read more about how the numbers are counted.

    At the top of the tab you also switch between its views: Overview, Dishes, Staff and Rank. What follows is about Overview.

    The main numbers

    You'll see 5 tiles. Changes compare with the previous period, and the first 2 tiles also have a small day-by-day line.

    • New reviews: the reviews that came in during the period.
    • Average: the mean of the new reviews' stars.
    • Negative: the new reviews of 1, 2 or 3 stars. Below it you see how many were serious, meaning 1 or 2 stars.
    • Waiting for a reply: reviews whose reply we've written and nobody has looked at yet. It's the same number as on the Replies tab.
    • Reply time. This shows the hours between a 1 to 3-star review and the owner's reply. You see the middle value, or median. Below it is the share of negative reviews answered, both compared with the previous period.

    Reply times for replies closed by hand are counted separately. A reply dated at or before its review, or more than 1 year later, is also left out of the median. You see a separate count of replies with uncertain times. Negative reviews written before the venue's first report are counted separately too. With fewer than 5 eligible replies, the tile says Kevés adat, meaning there is too little data.

    Below the tile, you also see the percentage of replies sent unchanged and how many replies that represents. Replies marked as posted by hand are left out of this figure.

    The first 3 tiles open the reviews behind them, and Waiting for a reply opens the Replies tab. If some of the period's reviews haven't been analysed yet, a note under the tiles tells you how many.

    Every tile and the table have a ?. Open it to read what the number means, which days it covers in the account's time zone, what counts towards it and when it was last checked. It also explains why the count can differ from Google's public total.

    What's usual for your account

    Below the new and negative review counts and Reply time, you'll also see a comparison with your account's own previous 90 days. A separate note marks a difference of at least 20% above or below that level. With fewer than 30 reviews in those 90 days, the line says there's no usual figure yet. Other restaurants' data doesn't count towards it.

    Per venue

    The Éttermenként section, meaning per venue, gives each venue one row. For the selected period, it shows these figures.

    • The new review count alongside the previous period's count.
    • The average rating of the new reviews and its change.
    • The share of 1- and 2-star reviews and its change.
    • The breakdown by star alongside the previous period's breakdown.
    • The share of negative reviews answered and the median reply time.

    The Google-értékelés (Google rating) column is the public, cumulative figure, with the Változás (change) column beside it. It's separate from the average of the period's new reviews. The line shows the last 30 days, or the last 90 for a 90-day period. It uses the venue's own scale, and the dot is today's value.

    A switched-off venue still appears if it had reviews in the period. Its name carries the Switched off tag, so a past period keeps the figures for the venues that were part of it.

    A line under a venue's name can show how many days have passed without a review and the usual gap between reviews. It appears after at least 1 week and at least 3 times the usual gap without a new review. The usual gap comes from the venue's own last 90 days, up to the end of the period, with at least 10 reviews.

    The venue whose rating dropped most comes first. On a computer, click a column heading to sort it another way. On a phone, venues appear as cards, with a sort control above them.

    If you have one venue

    The per-venue table and the Leginkább line, meaning where a theme comes up most, are hidden. Instead, the bars under Hetente, meaning weekly, show the weeks alongside the previous period. You'll also see the star breakdown, the Reply time tile and the lines comparing your figures with what's usual.

    Themes

    We read the reviews with text to see what they're about, like the service, the wait or the prices. Under What guests write about you'll find two lists, What guests praise and What guests complain about. Each shows the 10 most common themes.

    • For each theme you see how many of 100 reviews mentioned it, and how that changed since the period before.
    • If a theme comes up far more at one venue than at the others, the “Most at …” line names it.
    • If a theme came up fewer than 5 times, we say there's too little data instead of showing a change.
    • A review counts towards a theme only once.
    • A theme counts as a complaint when it comes up in a review with a negative tone, or in one rated 3 stars or fewer.
    • It counts as praise when it comes up in a review with a positive tone, or in one rated 4 stars or more.

    Each theme also has a small line showing its rate per 100 reviews over the last 12 weeks. A week with fewer than 5 reviews leaves a gap.

    Each theme gets one consistent name, so you can compare themes across venues and languages.

    Staff mentions

    Staff guests mention by name shows the 6 staff members mentioned most, spelled the way guests wrote them. One name at one venue is one person. Each name shows how many mentions it had and what share of them was praise. Here a mention counts as praise when it's in a 4-star or 5-star review. The All, per venue link takes you to the Staff view, as Staff and name mentions explains.

    Downloading and printing

    Use Download as spreadsheet at the per-venue table, either theme list or the people list to save the rows. They keep the order you see on screen, and the file name starts with your account's name.

    You'll also find these in Letöltés, the download menu at the top. As an owner, you can reach the daily numbers CSV, reviews CSV and JSON export here too. Their file names also start with your account's name. Read more about downloading your data.

    Printable summary puts the period on one A4 page. Each venue also has a Konyhai kártya link for a kitchen-board card. Printable summary explains what they contain and how to print them.
