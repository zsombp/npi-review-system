The daily report

It arrives at the hour you set. On Telegram if you connect it, and always in your account.

What is in it

  • the number of new reviews
  • the star breakdown
  • the day's average
  • the venue's Google rating and total review count
  • the unanswered negative reviews of the last fourteen days
  • any venue no fresh data came back from

A review is new when the guest posted it in the twenty-four hours before the report.

Every message we sent is kept: the Sent messages page shows what went out, where and when.

In the help centre: Setting up the daily report

Every review in one place

Every review in one place, searchable by venue and by star.

One guest, one review. When a guest edits a review, Google gives it a new identifier. The report lists that change in a section of its own, from what to what, and does not count it again.

  • Edit watch

    You see when a guest rewrites a review afterwards, and what changed into what.

  • Listing watch

    If Google takes reviews off your profile, you see it in the report.

  • Alert

    We tell you when one-star reviews come in a run at a venue, or its average slips.

You approve every reply

The replies wait in a queue, the worst review first. You read it, rewrite it if you need to, and approve it.

With the Rewrite buttons you can ask for it shorter, warmer or firmer, or switch it to formal or informal address. You can undo it for eight seconds.

If you have added us as a manager on Google, we post it within a few minutes. If not, you copy it with one button, and we open Google for you.

Auto-answer with any package

By default, only positive reviews get a reply that nobody reads first. One-star and two-star reviews get one only if you turn that on.

What language do you write the reply in?
In the language the guest wrote in.
Can a reply go out without me knowing?
No. You approve every reply. Auto-answer is switched on separately, and by default it only answers positive reviews.

Try it: approve a reply.

Analysis

You see what keeps repeating: themes, venues side by side, times of day. We read out what keeps repeating in what guests write, and what they say about each dish.

  • Themes: what repeats in what guests write, and how often
  • Your venues compared with each other
  • Negatives by time of day: lunch, afternoon, evening
  • Guest mix: which languages your guests write in
  • Staff mentions: who gets praised by name, and how often
  • Dish mentions: what guests write about each dish
  • Rate and rank: how many reviews arrive, and where you stand in search

Weekly summary

On Monday, shortly after the daily report, the weekly summary goes out. It sets the past week against the week before, venue by venue: the direction of the change, the staff mentioned most, the themes that keep coming back and the language mix.

Team

You invite your colleagues and decide who sees which venue.

  • Owner

    Sees and does everything a member does. On top of that, invites and removes people, adds and switches off venues, changes the settings, and manages the search phrases.

  • Member

    Sees every page and can work on the replies, but cannot change the account settings.

Tripadvisor

Paste in the venue's Tripadvisor address, and those reviews come in too, kept apart.

The assistant

You can ask it questions. It answers from your own reviews and says how many it read.

Where there is no data, it says so.

On your phone

You can put it on your phone's home screen, and it opens like an app.

It runs in the browser, on your phone and on a computer.
