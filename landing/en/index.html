How it starts

Four steps. The third one needs your Google account. The rest need nothing.

  1. You sign up

    You give an email address and a password. The trial runs for 14 days, and we do not invoice during it.

  2. You add the venue

    You type the name and the street, and we find the Google Business Profile. One account holds as many venues as you need.

  3. You add us as a manager

    On the Google Business Profile you add our account as a manager. From then on we read the reviews and post the replies you approved. You can take that access back at any time.

  4. The first report arrives tomorrow morning

    The first daily report comes the next morning. After that it comes every day at the hour you set.

What the plans contain

Three plans. Monitoring, the daily report and the replies are in all three. What changes is how much we read out of what your guests wrote.
