The product

The daily message

One message arrives every morning in the Telegram chat or group you name. Per venue: how many reviews came in, the star breakdown, the day's average and the current Google rating. At the end, the negative reviews nobody has answered yet.

That message is the product. The account exists so you can approve drafts, look back at history and manage your venues, not so you open it every day.

A red dot marks a venue that took a one or two star review that day. If a venue could not be checked, the message says so, because a zero there means missing data rather than a quiet day.

Three things

What BistroTech does

Daily monitoring

We check the Google Business Profile of every venue each day and collect the new reviews into a single message. It lands in a channel you already read. No email per review, and nothing you have to open to find out what happened.

Reply drafts

Every review gets a drafted reply in the venue's own tone. By default a person approves it: reads it, edits it, and only then does it go to Google. Fully automatic answering is a separate module you switch on yourself, and the consequence is spelled out at the moment you switch it on.

Reporting

Rating trend per venue, the themes that recur in what guests write, venues compared against each other, and the staff named in reviews, quoted verbatim. Not a sentence instead of the data, but what is actually in the text.

The interface

What it looks like

There is an interface as well as the daily message. You open it to approve a reply, to look back over a month, or to read what guests actually wrote. Three screens from it.

The screenshots come from the BistroTech demo account. The venue names, the guest texts and the numbers are invented, and no real restaurant's data appears in them. The interface is shown in Hungarian, which is the language the service runs in; it also runs in English.

Getting started

How onboarding works

Four steps. We do most of the work and stay with you through it.

  1. You add your venues

    You tell us which places to watch. We find and match the Google Business Profile from the name and address.

  2. You add our manager account

    You add our manager account to each Google Business Profile with Manager permission. That is how we read the reviews and publish the approved replies, through Google's own interface. You can revoke it at any time.

  3. We connect Telegram

    You tell us which chat or group the daily message should go to. Connecting takes a few minutes and we walk you through it.

  4. The first report arrives next morning

    The first daily message comes the following morning, and then every day at the same time.

Reference

In production across eleven venues

An eleven-venue Budapest restaurant group runs the system every day. That installation is where the product comes from: the daily monitoring runs there, the reports come from there, and every change works there before it works anywhere else.

We do not name the group or its venues, and we show no review content from them. That is our own decision, not a condition the customer imposed.
