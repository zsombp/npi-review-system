How it starts

Four steps. A few minutes after you add the venue, you can see your reviews. Google access is set up separately, and only posting replies needs it.

  1. 1

    You sign up

    You give an email address and a password. The trial runs for 14 days, and we do not invoice during it.

  2. 2

    You add the venue

    You find the venue by name. Within minutes you see its latest reviews and the first numbers. One account can hold several venues.

  3. 3

    You add us as a manager

    On your Google Business Profile, you add our account as a manager. From then on we can post the replies you approved. Until then, you copy the finished reply with one button and paste it in yourself. You can take the access back at any time.

  4. 4

    You set up the daily report

    You connect Telegram and pick the time. The first daily report arrives the next morning, then every day at the same time. It is in your account even if you connect nothing.

Common questions

More detail in the help centre. Open the help centre

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser, on your phone and on a computer. If you like, you can also put it on your phone's home screen.

Do you touch my Google Business Profile?

Only if you add us as a manager. Then we post the replies you approved. You can take the access back at any time.

Can a reply go out without me knowing?

No. You approve every reply. Auto-answer is switched on separately, and by default it only answers positive reviews.

What language do you write the reply in?

In the language the guest wrote in.

Do I need Telegram?

No. The report is in your account every morning as well. We send it to Telegram if you connect it.

How much does it cost?

We will publish the package prices soon. We do not invoice during the 14-day trial.

Can several venues go in one account?

Yes. Above ten venues, we go through the package with you in person.
