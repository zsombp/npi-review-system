Every venue, every day, in one report

The report shows, per venue, how many reviews came in at each star, five down to one. What you do with those numbers inside the restaurant is your own business.

Every message we sent is kept: the Sent messages page shows what went out, where and when.

Who sees what

You invite your colleagues and decide who sees which venue.

  • Owner

    Sees and does everything a member does. On top of that, invites and removes people, adds and switches off venues, changes the settings, and manages the search phrases.

  • Member

    Sees every page and can work on the replies, but cannot change the account settings.

Your venues side by side

You see what keeps repeating: themes, venues side by side, times of day.

  • Your venues compared with each other
  • Negatives by time of day: lunch, afternoon, evening
  • Themes: what repeats in what guests write, and how often

  • Alert

    We tell you when one-star reviews come in a run at a venue, or its average slips.

  • Group

    The Group page shows several accounts in one table: one line per account, with the new reviews, the average, the one-star reviews and what is waiting for a reply. It is worth having when you run more than one account.

  • Recognition

    The Recognition page holds the names guests wrote in their reviews. This list is for saying thank you, not for appraising anybody.

Eleven venues, every morning

BistroTech runs every morning at a Budapest group with eleven venues. That is where it started, and it has run live there ever since.

We do not name the group or its venues, and we show no reviews from them. That is our own decision, not a customer's request.

Common questions

More detail in the help centre. Open the help centre

Can several venues go in one account?

Yes. Above ten venues, we go through the package with you in person.

Do you touch my Google Business Profile?

Only if you add us as a manager. Then we post the replies you approved. You can take the access back at any time.

Can a reply go out without me knowing?

No. You approve every reply. Auto-answer is switched on separately, and by default it only answers positive reviews.
